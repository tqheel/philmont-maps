10:T2a1b,<h1 id="2026-philmont-trek--crew-618-j-trek-guide">2026 Philmont Trek — Crew 618-J Trek Guide</h1>
<p><strong>Expedition Number:</strong> 618-J
<strong>Trek:</strong> Itinerary 12-1 · Challenging · 54 miles · South Country
<strong>Start Date:</strong> June 18, 2026
<strong>Elevation range:</strong> 6,729 ft (minimum) to 9,343 ft (maximum)
<strong>Duration:</strong> 12 days
<strong>Trailhead:</strong> Rayado Trailhead (Day 2 departure from Base Camp by bus)
<strong>Return:</strong> Hike over Tooth of Time Ridge into Base Camp on Day 12</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="logistics-and-acclimatization-timeline">Logistics and Acclimatization Timeline</h2>
<p>Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) is a significant risk for crews arriving from lower elevations. Our crew follows a structured acclimatization schedule before stepping onto the trail.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>June 16, 2026:</strong> Depart Raleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU) on United flight UA 2270 to Denver, Colorado. Upon arrival, load gear into two reserved minivans and drive south to Colorado Springs.</li>
<li><strong>June 16–17, 2026:</strong> Two-night acclimatization layover at the Great Wolf Lodge, Colorado Springs (~6,035 ft). Active acclimatization includes the Garden of the Gods and the Manitou Incline.</li>
<li><strong>June 18, 2026:</strong> Drive the final stretch to Cimarron, NM. Arrive for check-in at Philmont Base Camp (Day 1).</li>
<li><strong>June 30, 2026:</strong> Return to Denver and fly home to RDU on United flight UA 2671.</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="executive-summary">Executive Summary</h2>
<p>This is a 12-day South Country loop — Philmont&#39;s classic southern circuit. The route follows Rayado Creek up to Fish Camp, swings west through Apache Springs territory, climbs to the South Country&#39;s hub at Beaubien for a two-night layover, pushes north to Black Mountain for historical programs and rock climbing at Miners Park, then arcs back southeast via Bear Caves and Urraca Mesa before finishing with the iconic Tooth of Time summit on the final day.</p>
<p>Key characteristics of this itinerary:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Primarily south country terrain:</strong> canyon corridors and open meadows below 9,000 ft for most of the trek — more forgiving on altitude acclimatization than north country routes</li>
<li><strong>Two major layover camps:</strong> Beaubien (2 nights with showers and cowboy programming) and Miners Park (rock climbing day)</li>
<li><strong>Diverse program portfolio:</strong> every major program category is represented — living history, equestrian, challenge/ropes, archery, logging heritage, and technical climbing</li>
<li><strong>One dry camp on Day 11</strong> at Stockade Ridge — classified dry (no developed water system) but a natural water source exists at camp per the official itinerary</li>
<li><strong>Fitness note:</strong> The approach to Black Mountain on Day 8 (via the south-face trail of Black Mountain) is rated among the most difficult single-day ascents on the ranch; prepare the crew mentally for it</li>
<li><strong>Final push:</strong> Day 12 is emotionally high but physically demanding — Tooth of Time is a Class 3 scramble after a full morning of hiking</li>
</ul>
